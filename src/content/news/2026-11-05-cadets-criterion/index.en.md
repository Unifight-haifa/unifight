Today the Cadets Criterion took place: a national ranking tournament of great importance that affects the athletes' national ranking, selection for the Israeli national team and participation in the European tournament circuit.

Our club's athletes showed impressive ability and outstanding results, finishing the competition with 11 medals and first place in the overall ranking among all judo clubs in Israel!

We are very proud of all our athletes for their fighting spirit, determination, commitment and the hard work that brings results!

> First place in Israel is the result of daily work, perseverance and belief in the path.

This is only the start of the season, and we are looking ahead to the next challenges on the national and international stage!

UNIFIGHT HAIFA JUDO CLUB
